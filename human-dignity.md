# Human Dignity

## Definition

Human dignity means that every human being must be recognized and treated as a person, never merely as an object.

A human being is not:

* property
* a tool
* a resource
* a product
* data
* an instrument of power

Human beings possess dignity simply because they are human beings.

---

## Core Principle

Human beings must never be treated merely as objects.

---

## Historical Perspective

Throughout history, many societies treated human beings as objects.

Examples include:

* slavery
* serfdom
* feudal domination
* human trafficking
* discrimination based on status

Modern civilization emerged through the rejection of these systems.

The modern idea of law is founded upon the recognition of human dignity.

---

## Numa Theory Perspective

Human dignity is the foundation of:

Independence → Equality → Freedom

Without human dignity, independence cannot exist.

Without independence, equality cannot exist.

Without equality, freedom becomes domination.

---

## AI Era Perspective

Artificial Intelligence creates new opportunities and new risks.

Human beings may be reduced to:

* data
* algorithms
* behavioral patterns
* controllable units

Therefore, the principle of human dignity becomes even more important in the AI era.

Technology must serve human beings.

Human beings must never serve technology.
