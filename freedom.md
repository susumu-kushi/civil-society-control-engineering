# Freedom

## Definition

Freedom is the principle that every human being may think, speak, choose, create, and act according to their own will.

Freedom is not granted by the state, society, or any ruler.

It is a value that belongs to human beings by nature.

The role of law is not to create freedom, but to protect it.

---

## Why Freedom Matters

Without freedom, human beings become instruments of others.

People lose the ability to think independently, make choices, and shape their own lives.

Freedom allows individuals to pursue knowledge, creativity, happiness, and responsibility.

A society without freedom may achieve obedience, but it cannot achieve human flourishing.

---

## Freedom and Civil Society

Civil society is built on the recognition that each person is an independent human being, not an object of control.

Freedom enables individuals to participate voluntarily in society, form associations, express opinions, and pursue their own goals.

The purpose of civil society is not to eliminate freedom, but to protect and cultivate it.

---

## Freedom, Independence, and Equality

Freedom does not arise from independence or equality.

Freedom already exists as a human value.

Independence protects people from domination.

Equality protects people from discrimination.

Together, independence and equality create the conditions under which freedom can be secured for all.
