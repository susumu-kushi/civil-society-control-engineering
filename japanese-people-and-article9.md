# For Japanese People Torn by the National Anthem, National Flag, and Article 9

## Author

Susumu Kushi

---

## Summary

Why do many Japanese people feel conflicted about the National Anthem, the National Flag, the Emperor System, and Article 9 of the Constitution?

This book argues that the answer cannot be found simply through political labels such as conservative versus progressive, right versus left, or nationalism versus anti-nationalism.

Instead, these debates should be understood through a historical and philosophical framework:

Feudalism → Modernity → Civil Society

Using Numa Theory, the book analyzes the tensions between historical traditions, modern constitutional principles, and the unfinished development of civil society in Japan.

---

## Core Question

Why do Japanese people continue to feel uncertainty and conflict regarding the state, national symbols, and constitutional principles?

## Central Message

The problem is not the anthem.

The problem is not the flag.

The problem is not Article 9.

The deeper question is:

What kind of society are we trying to build?

Japan is not returning to feudalism.

Nor is it yet a fully developed civil society.

It remains a society in transition.
