# Introduction to a Constitution for Earth Citizens by AI

## Author

Susumu Kushi

---

## Summary

This book explores a fundamental question for the future of humanity:

Can human society move beyond the framework of nation-states?

Modern states liberated individuals from feudal status systems and established the principles of citizenship, constitutional government, and fundamental rights.

However, modern society also created new boundaries through nationality and national borders.

This book examines whether the principles of human dignity, equality, freedom, and peace can be expanded from national citizenship to Earth citizenship.

Using the Constitution of Japan as a starting point, the book proposes a new perspective:

From Nationals to Earth Citizens.

---

## Core Question

Can humanity create a civil society that extends beyond national borders?
