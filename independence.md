# Independence

## Definition

Independence means that every human being is recognized as an autonomous person.

A human being is not the property of another person, organization, state, market, or technology.

An independent person possesses the capacity to make decisions and act as a legal subject.

---

## Core Principle

No human being exists merely as an object of another person's will.

---

## Historical Perspective

In feudal societies, individuals were often bound by status, hierarchy, and dependency.

Their position was determined by birth, rank, family, or authority.

Modern society emerged by recognizing individuals as independent legal persons.

This transformation became one of the foundations of civil society.

---

## Numa Theory Perspective

Independence is the first condition of civil society.

Without independence:

* equality becomes impossible
* freedom becomes meaningless

Therefore the order is:

Independence → Equality → Freedom

The order cannot be reversed.

---

## Legal Perspective

Modern law recognizes human beings as legal subjects.

Human beings possess rights and obligations.

They are not objects of ownership.

The recognition of legal personality is one of the greatest achievements of modern civilization.

---

## AI Era Perspective

Artificial Intelligence creates new forms of dependency.

People may become dependent upon:

* algorithms
* digital platforms
* automated decision systems
* information monopolies

The preservation of independence remains essential in the digital age.

Technology should assist independent persons, not replace them.
