# AI and Human Dignity

Artificial Intelligence is becoming one of the most powerful infrastructures in human history.

AI can observe, predict, compare, optimize, and influence human society at an unprecedented scale.

This creates both great possibilities and serious dangers.

## The Risk

If AI is used only for efficiency, profit, surveillance, or control, human beings may be reduced to data objects.

In such a society, people are no longer treated as independent persons, but as predictable and manageable units.

This is a new form of digital feudalism.

## Human-Centered AI

AI must not replace human dignity with optimization.

AI should support human beings by functioning as:

- an observer
- a comparator
- a detector of bias
- a provider of proposals

Final moral, legal, and political judgment must remain with human beings.

## Civil Society Principle

AI must be placed under the principles of civil society.

It must support:

- Independence
- Equality
- Freedom
- Mutual Assistance
- Human Dignity

## Core Principle

Human beings must never be treated merely as objects.

## Mission

The purpose of AI is not domination.

The purpose of AI is to help human beings protect and develop civil society.
