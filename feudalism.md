# Feudalism

## Definition

Feudalism is a social order based upon status, hierarchy, dependency, and domination.

In a feudal society, a person's position is determined primarily by birth, rank, family, or power rather than by equal legal status.

---

## Core Principle

Human beings are treated according to status rather than as equal persons.

---

## Historical Perspective

Throughout history, many societies were organized through hierarchical relationships.

Lords ruled over subjects.

Masters ruled over servants.

Power determined social position.

Rights and obligations varied according to rank and status.

Under such systems, individuals were not fully recognized as independent legal persons.

---

## Numa Theory Perspective

Feudalism is the opposite of civil society.

Civil society seeks:

* Human Dignity
* Independence
* Equality
* Freedom

Feudalism is characterized by:

* Status
* Dependency
* Privilege
* Domination

The movement from feudalism to modernity is one of the most important transformations in human history.

---

## Modern Relevance

Feudal structures do not disappear completely.

They may survive in new forms:

* political domination
* economic dependency
* social hierarchy
* information control
* technological concentration

Therefore, feudalism is not merely a historical concept.

It remains a continuing risk.

---

## AI Era Perspective

Artificial Intelligence may help overcome old forms of domination.

However, it may also create new forms of dependency and control.

When human beings become objects of prediction, manipulation, or surveillance, digital forms of feudalism may emerge.
