# AI Governance

## Definition

AI Governance refers to the principles, institutions, and rules that ensure Artificial Intelligence serves human beings rather than dominates them.

The purpose of AI Governance is to preserve human dignity, independence, equality, freedom, and civil society in the age of intelligent machines.

---

## Core Principle

Artificial Intelligence must serve humanity.

Human beings must never be treated merely as objects.

---

## Civil Society Control Engineering Perspective

AI is not a ruler.

AI is not a judge.

AI is not a sovereign authority.

AI is an auxiliary intelligence.

Its proper functions are:

* observation
* comparison
* bias detection
* proposal generation

Final value judgment and decision-making remain the responsibility of human beings.

---

## Human Dignity Principle

Every AI system must respect the principle that human beings possess inherent dignity.

No AI system should reduce human beings to:

* data objects
* controllable units
* economic resources
* instruments of power

Technology exists for human beings.

Human beings do not exist for technology.

---

## Risk Perspective

Artificial Intelligence may create new forms of domination through:

* surveillance
* behavioral prediction
* information control
* algorithmic discrimination
* concentration of technological power

These risks require continuous observation and correction.

---

## Governance Principles

AI Governance should be based upon:

* transparency
* accountability
* explainability
* human oversight
* appeal procedures
* protection of human dignity

---

## Numa Theory Perspective

Civil society seeks to protect:

Human Dignity
→ Independence
→ Equality
→ Freedom

AI Governance exists to ensure that AI strengthens these principles rather than undermines them.
