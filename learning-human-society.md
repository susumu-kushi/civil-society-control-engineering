## Overview

*Learning Human Society Together* is an introductory book for young readers.

It explains the foundations of human society in simple language and encourages children to think about how people can live together peacefully and responsibly.

The book introduces essential concepts that support modern civil society and prepares readers to understand more advanced topics such as Civil Society Control Engineering and Numa Theory.

---

## Core Question

What does it mean to live as a human being in society?

---

## Core Principle

Human beings must never be treated as objects.

Every person possesses dignity and should be respected as an independent individual.

## Main Themes

### School as a Small Human Society

Children learn not only reading and mathematics, but also cooperation, responsibility, and respect for others.

### Human Beings Are Not Objects

Every person has dignity and must never be treated merely as a tool or possession.

### Competition and Mutual Aid

A healthy society requires both free competition and mutual support.

### Freedom and Responsibility

Freedom is not doing whatever one wants.

Freedom requires respect for others and responsibility for one's actions.

### The State and Law

The state exists to protect society, but state power must also be limited by law.

### AI and the Future

AI is a powerful tool.

However, AI should assist human beings, not replace human judgment.

## Human–AI Relationship

AI can:

* Observe
* Analyze
* Compare
* Assist

Human beings must:

* Judge
* Choose
* Take responsibility

A central principle of the book is:

**AI assists. Human beings decide.**
