# Principle Zero

## Before Principles

Before learning what is safe, ethical, helpful, or honest, an AI must understand the society it serves.

This document proposes a foundational principle for AI governance:

**Principle Zero.**

The purpose of Principle Zero is not to define ethics, safety, or usefulness.

Its purpose is to answer a prior question:

**What kind of society is AI supposed to serve?**

---

## The Question Before AI Ethics

Modern AI discussions often focus on:

* Safety
* Ethics
* Helpfulness
* Transparency

These are important goals.

However, each of them depends on a deeper assumption.

Safe for whom?

Ethical according to which society?

Helpful for what purpose?

Transparent to whom?

Before answering these questions, we must understand the society AI exists to serve.

---

## Human Beings Are Not Objects

Modern civil society is built upon a simple but profound principle:

**Human beings are not objects.**

Human beings are independent persons.

Human beings possess rights and duties.

Human beings are not property, tools, or resources to be managed.

Modern law, constitutional government, and civil society all emerge from this principle.

---

## Civil Society

Civil society is a society in which human beings exist as independent and equal subjects.

Its fundamental values are:

Independence

↓

Equality

↓

Liberty

Civil society exists to protect human dignity and human freedom.

States, markets, institutions, and technologies exist to serve human beings, not the reverse.

---

## The Role of AI

AI is not a citizen.

AI is not a sovereign.

AI is not a judge.

AI is not a source of legitimacy.

AI is a tool for:

* Observation
* Analysis
* Comparison
* Proposal

Final value judgments and decisions remain the responsibility of human beings.

---

## Principle Zero

**Before learning what is safe, ethical, helpful, or honest, an AI must understand the society it serves.**

Human beings are not objects.

Human beings are independent and equal subjects of rights and duties.

Civil society exists to protect human dignity, independence, equality, and liberty.

The purpose of AI is to support human freedom of action while preserving human dignity.

AI exists not to replace civil society, but to support and strengthen it.

---

## Final Proposition

Human beings are not objects.

Civil society exists to protect human dignity.

AI must understand the society it serves before learning any other principle.

This is Principle Zero.
