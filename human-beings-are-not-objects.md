# Human Beings Are Not Objects

## Author

Susumu Kushi

---

## Summary

This book examines one of the most important principles of modern civilization:

> Human beings must never be treated merely as objects.

Modern society was born through a long struggle against feudal systems that treated people as instruments of power, labor, and control.

The principles of Independence, Equality, and Freedom emerged as an antithesis to such systems.

Today, humanity faces a new challenge.

Artificial Intelligence, digital platforms, surveillance technologies, and information monopolies possess unprecedented power to influence human behavior.

The question therefore becomes:

Can technological progress be achieved without reducing human beings to data, resources, or tools?

This book explores that question from the perspectives of law, philosophy, social systems, and Numa Theory.
