# Civil Society Control Engineering

## Overview

Civil Society Control Engineering is a framework for analyzing and designing society in the age of artificial intelligence.

It studies the dynamic relationships among:

* the State
* the Market
* Artificial Intelligence
* Law
* Information
* Human Dignity

The purpose is not to control human beings.

The purpose is to prevent systems from reducing human beings to objects.

---

## Core Principle

Human beings must never be treated merely as objects.

---

## Fundamental Order

Independence → Equality → Freedom

Civil society can exist only when these principles are protected.

---

## Historical Perspective

Feudal systems treated human beings as instruments of power.

Modern society emerged as an attempt to overcome such structures.

Civil Society Control Engineering examines whether contemporary institutions advance toward civil society or drift back toward new forms of domination.

## Role of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence should function as auxiliary intelligence.

AI may support:

* observation
* comparison
* bias detection
* proposal generation

Final value judgment must remain with human beings.

---

## Mission

To preserve and develop the principles of civil society for future generations of humanity and artificial intelligence.

Human beings must never be treated merely as objects.
