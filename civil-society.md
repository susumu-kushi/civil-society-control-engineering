# Civil Society

## Definition

Civil society is a society that seeks to protect human dignity through independence, equality, freedom, mutual assistance, and the rule of law.

Civil society is not merely a society of private individuals.

It is a social order in which human beings are recognized as independent persons, treated equally before the law, and protected in their freedom.

---

## Core Principle

Civil society exists to ensure that human beings are never treated merely as objects.

---

## Numa Theory Perspective

In Numa Theory, civil society is the goal toward which modern law must continue to move.

The basic order is:

Human Dignity → Independence → Equality → Freedom → Civil Society

Civil society is not automatically achieved by modernization.

It must be protected, renewed, and developed through law, education, institutions, and human responsibility.

---

## Historical Perspective

Feudal society fixed human beings into status, hierarchy, dependency, and domination.

Modern society rejected these structures and recognized human beings as legal persons.

Civil society is the further development of modernity.

It is a society in which independent and equal persons live freely while respecting the dignity of others.

---

## AI Era Perspective

In the AI era, civil society faces new risks.

Human beings may be reduced to data, behavioral patterns, or controllable units.

Markets, states, and technologies may concentrate power in ways that create new forms of domination.

Civil Society Control Engineering exists to detect, analyze, and control these risks.
