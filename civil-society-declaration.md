# Civil Society Declaration

## Preamble

Human beings must never be treated merely as objects.

No person shall be reduced to a tool of power, labor, profit, ideology, technology, or control.

Civil society exists to protect the dignity of every human being.

The purpose of law, institutions, markets, and technology is not domination, but the development of independent human beings living together in freedom and mutual respect.

## Declaration

We affirm the following principles:

### 1. Human Dignity

Every human being possesses inherent dignity.

No authority, organization, market, or technology may deny that dignity.

### 2. Independence

Human beings must be recognized as independent persons.

Dependence upon power must never become a condition for human worth.

### 3. Equality

All persons must be treated equally before the law.

Privileges based solely upon status, birth, wealth, or power are contrary to civil society.

### 4. Freedom

Freedom is meaningful only when independence and equality are protected.

Freedom belongs to all, not merely to the powerful.

### 5. Mutual Assistance

Civil society is strengthened when human beings cooperate and assist one another.

Strength exists to protect weakness, not to exploit it.

### 6. Responsibility

Freedom requires responsibility.

Every generation bears the responsibility to preserve and improve civil society for those who follow.

## AI Era Commitment

Artificial Intelligence must serve humanity.

AI must never become a tool for reducing human beings to objects of prediction, manipulation, or control.

The final responsibility for moral judgment, law, and public decision-making remains with human beings.

## Closing Statement

Civil society is not inherited automatically.

It must be protected, renewed, and defended by every generation.

Human beings must never be treated merely as objects.
