# Lecture 0

# Why Human Beings Are Not Objects

## なぜ人間は物ではないのか

Civil Society Control Engineering begins with one simple principle.

Human beings are not objects.

人間は物ではない。

A human being is not a tool, not a resource, not data, and not a component of a system.

A human being is a subject.

近代市民社会は、すべての人間を「物」ではなく、独立・平等の権利義務の主体として認めるところから始まる。

In feudal society, human beings were often treated according to status, bloodline, ownership, obedience, or service.

Modern civil society rejects this structure.

The purpose of law, education, and technology must therefore be judged by one question:

Does it protect human beings as subjects?

Or does it reduce them to objects?

## Human Beings and Civil Society

Civil society begins when human beings are recognized as subjects rather than objects.

A subject possesses rights and duties.

A subject can own property, enter contracts, express opinions, and participate in society through free will.

An object cannot.

市民社会とは、人間を主体として扱う社会である。

封建社会とは、人間を支配や従属の対象として扱う社会である。

The distinction between subject and object is therefore the starting point of modern law.

## Why This Principle Matters Today

Artificial Intelligence has dramatically increased the power to collect, analyze, predict, and influence human behavior.

If technology is allowed to treat human beings merely as data resources, society may gradually return to a new form of feudalism.

Civil Society Control Engineering therefore asks a simple but fundamental question:

How can law, education, and technology protect human beings as subjects?

This question forms the foundation of the entire framework.

## Core Principle

Human beings must never be treated merely as objects.

人間は、決して単なる物として扱われてはならない。

This is the foundation of Civil Society Control Engineering.
