# Lecture 3

# Education for the Subject

## 主体を育てる教育

Education is not the installation of information into a human being.

Education is not the production of useful components for society.

教育とは、人間に知識を詰め込むことではない。

教育とは、社会の部品を生産することでもない。

The purpose of education is to nurture human beings as subjects.

教育の目的は、人間を主体として育てることにある。

## 1. Human Dignity Comes First

Every human being possesses dignity.

Human dignity is not granted by the state.

It is not granted by society.

It is not granted by the market.

It is not granted by AI.

It exists before all of them.

人間は、もともと尊厳を持つ存在である。

教育は、その尊厳を発見し、自覚するための営みである。

A person who understands human dignity understands that he or she is not an object.

A person is a subject.

This is the starting point of education.

## 2. Education and Civil Society

Civil society cannot survive if its members cease to think.

When people stop thinking, they become vulnerable to manipulation.

They follow authority blindly.

They follow crowds blindly.

They follow algorithms blindly.

Such a society gradually abandons independence, equality, and freedom.

Therefore, education must cultivate the capacity for independent judgment.

教育とは、自ら考える力を育てることである。

The goal is not obedience.

The goal is not conformity.

The goal is responsible freedom.

## 3. Education in the Age of AI

Artificial Intelligence can assist learning.

It can explain.

It can summarize.

It can compare.

It can analyze.

But AI must not replace human judgment.

AI must not replace human responsibility.

AI must not replace human freedom.

AI may support thinking.

AI must not think in place of the human being.

AIは主体を支援する道具でなければならない。

## 4. Questions Every Citizen Must Ask

In the age of AI, education must teach citizens to ask questions.

Why am I seeing this information?

Who benefits from this recommendation?

What assumptions are hidden in this analysis?

Am I making a decision?

Or is someone making a decision for me?

These questions help preserve human independence.

## 5. Education and Self-Government

The highest purpose of education is not employment.

It is not economic productivity.

It is not technological efficiency.

The highest purpose of education is self-government.

教育の最高目的は、自らを統治できる主体を育てることである。

A person who governs himself or herself is less likely to become the object of domination.

Such people form the foundation of civil society.

## The Educational Sequence

Civil Society Control Engineering understands education through the following sequence:

Human Dignity

↓

Independence

↓

Equality

↓

Freedom

↓

Responsible Citizenship

人間尊厳

↓

独立

↓

平等

↓

自由

↓

市民としての責任

This sequence connects education to the broader structure of civil society.

## Core Principle

Education nurtures the subject.

教育は主体を育てる。

Its purpose is not to create useful objects.

Its purpose is to help human beings remain independent, equal, and free subjects throughout their lives.

法は主体を守る。

教育は主体を育てる。

AIは主体を支援する道具でなければならない。
