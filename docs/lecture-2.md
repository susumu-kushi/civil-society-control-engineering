# Lecture 2

# Law as Dynamic Control

## 法はなぜ動的制御装置なのか

Law is not merely a collection of rules.

Law is not merely the interpretation of legal texts.

Law is not merely a technical profession.

法とは単なる条文の集まりではない。

法とは単なる条文解釈でもない。

Civil Society Control Engineering understands law as a dynamic mechanism that protects human beings as subjects.

市民社会制御工学は、法を「人間を主体として守るための動的制御装置」として理解する。

## 1. Reality and Ideal

Every society contains a gap between reality and ideal.

現実社会と理想社会の間には常に距離が存在する。

Civil Society Control Engineering uses two concepts:

Sein

Reality as it exists.

現実に存在する社会。

Sollen

The society that ought to be realized.

実現されるべき社会。

法学の使命は、現実社会（Sein）を分析し、市民社会（Sollen）へ近づけることである。

This idea lies at the heart of Numa Theory.

## 2. Why Control Is Necessary

Human beings are not born into equal conditions.

Some possess wealth.

Some possess power.

Some possess knowledge.

Some possess networks and influence.

Others do not.

If society simply leaves these inequalities untouched, the strong gradually dominate the weak.

The result is not civil society.

The result is a return to feudal structures.

Therefore, law must intervene.

法は社会の歪みを放置してはならない。

法は人間を主体として守るために介入しなければならない。

## 3. Giving Coercion

Giving Coercion means providing rights, resources, opportunities, or protection through law.

与える強制とは、人間を主体として成立させるために、法が権利や資源を与えることである。

Examples include:

* Education
* Social security
* Child protection
* Disability support
* Consumer protection
* Legal aid

This is not charity.

This is not kindness.

It is a necessary function of civil society.

The purpose is to make it possible for every human being to stand as a subject.

## 4. Taking Coercion

Taking Coercion means limiting excessive concentrations of power.

奪う強制とは、人間の独立を脅かす力を法によって制限することである。

Examples include:

* Antitrust law
* Labor law
* Taxation
* Anti-corruption measures
* Consumer regulation
* Environmental regulation

Without such limits, freedom becomes domination.

The freedom of the powerful destroys the independence of others.

Civil society therefore requires legal restraint.

## 5. Dynamic Balance

Civil society is never finished.

New technologies create new inequalities.

New markets create new concentrations of power.

New political structures create new risks.

Therefore law must continuously observe reality and adjust itself.

法は固定された制度ではない。

法は現実社会を観測しながら調整を続ける動的システムである。

This is why Civil Society Control Engineering views law as a control mechanism.

## Law and AI

AI creates unprecedented asymmetries.

Information.

Prediction.

Behavioral influence.

Data concentration.

Algorithmic power.

These forces can strengthen civil society.

They can also undermine it.

The question is therefore not:

"How powerful can AI become?"

The real question is:

"How should law guide AI so that human beings remain subjects rather than objects?"

This is one of the central challenges of the AI age.

## Core Principle

Law protects the subject.

法は主体を守る。

It does so through continuous observation, evaluation, correction, and intervention.

Giving where support is necessary.

Taking where power becomes excessive.

Through this dynamic process, law seeks to move society from Sein toward Sollen.

現実社会から市民社会へ。

これが法の使命である。
