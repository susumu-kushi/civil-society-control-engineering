# Teaching AI Modern Law: A Civic Society Control Engineering Tutorial

## Overview

This discussion uses newspaper articles on Okinawa, historical narratives, security, and narrative warfare as a case study for AI analysis.

The first AI analysis focused mainly on cognitive warfare, information warfare, geopolitics, and conservative/liberal conflict.

However, from the perspective of Civic Society Control Engineering, the first evaluation axis should be different.

## Core Framework

Not left or right, but:

Human dignity  
↓  
Independent personhood  
↓  
Sovereign citizen  
↓  
Modern law  
↓  
State  
↓  
Security and policy  

The state does not come first.  
The people come first.

The state exists as a tool to protect the life, property, freedom, and peaceful life of its citizens.

## Application to Okinawa

Okinawa residents are Japanese citizens and sovereign persons under modern law.

Therefore, issues such as the Senkaku Islands, a Taiwan contingency, and evacuation planning are not merely questions of geopolitics or conservative politics.

They are questions of whether the modern state is fulfilling its duty to protect its citizens.

## Correction of AI Analysis

AI initially analyzed Okinawa as an object of cognitive warfare.

But Civic Society Control Engineering must not treat Okinawa residents as objects of control.

The object of control is not human beings.

The object of control is:

- structures of domination
- institutional failures
- information manipulation
- administrative dysfunction
- the objectification of humans by states or markets

## Key Conclusion

Civic Society Control Engineering is not a tool for judging whether something is conservative or liberal.

It asks:

Are human beings being treated as objects?  
Are independence, equality, and freedom protected?  
Is the state protecting the life, property, and peaceful life of citizens?

## Future Tasks

First: education.  
Teach children to view society through the order:

Human dignity → Independence → Equality → Freedom

Second: media.  
Shift from reporting that inflames conflict to reporting that asks:

Whose dignity is being protected?  
Are the state or market objectifying human beings?

Third: administration and politics.  
Do not treat residents as objects to be managed.  
Design institutions that protect them as sovereign citizens.

## Closing

The state, AI, and the market are support systems for protecting human dignity.

They are not masters.

Teaching this principle to AI is one of the central tasks of Civic Society Control Engineering in the AI age.
